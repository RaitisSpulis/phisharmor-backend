# PhishArmor Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: September 2026**

PhishArmor ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle text data, phone numbers, and screenshots within the PhishArmor mobile application.

### 1. Information Collection and Usage
- **Manual Checking:** When you input text, a phone number, or a screenshot for manual analysis, this data is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our remote server for real-time cyber security verification.
- **Background Protection (Premium):** If background screening is enabled, incoming SMS texts and caller IDs are intercepted locally on your device and transmitted securely to our server to determine threat levels.
- **Data Erasure:** We process your requests temporarily to generate safety reports. We DO NOT store, retain, or share your messages, screenshots, or personal identity profiles on our servers. All processed content is immediately flushed out after the security response is generated.

### 2. Permissions Required
To provide automated security, the app requires:
- `RECEIVE_SMS` & `READ_SMS` (To analyze text components for links)
- `READ_PHONE_STATE` (To verify incoming calling numbers against spam index lists)

### 3. Third-Party Services
We utilize high-level AI text analysis models (OpenAI API) to interpret incoming metadata and formatting rules. No tracking cookies or behavioral commercial profiling assets are embedded.

### 4. Data Security
All communications between the PhishArmor application and our hosting servers are encrypted using standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Rate limits are actively enforced to protect endpoint integrity.

### 5. Contact Us
For any privacy-related questions, please contact the developer channel.
